Chicken Ramen Bowl

A warming Japanese-inspired noodle soup with a rich broth, tender chicken, soft-boiled egg and spring onions.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 200g ramen noodles
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 soft-boiled eggs
  • Soy sauce, ginger, garlic
  • Spring onions & sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Boil broth with ginger and garlic for 10 minutes.
  2. Cook chicken in the broth until tender, then shred.
  3. Cook noodles separately as per packet instructions.
  4. Assemble bowl: noodles → broth → chicken → egg → toppings.
